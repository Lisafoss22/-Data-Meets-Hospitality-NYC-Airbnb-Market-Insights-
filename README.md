Thrilled to share a recent project where my team and I explored over 48,000 Airbnb listings across New York City using Tableau dashboards. Our goal? To uncover how pricing, availability, and guest satisfaction vary across neighborhoods and property types.

🔍 Key insights we discovered: ✔️ Service quality (especially cleanliness) matters more than price
✔️ Private rooms offer the best balance of affordability and satisfaction
✔️ Outer boroughs like Staten Island outperform in review scores but remain underutilized
✔️ Niche listings like Castles and Chalets earned near-perfect guest ratings
✔️ Pricing does not always equal better availability

📈 Our dashboards helped visualize trends like booking growth, room-type distribution, and city-level cleanliness metrics — enabling smarter, data-driven recommendations for hosts and platform managers.

🧠 This project reinforced how powerful data visualization can be in unlocking decisions that boost occupancy, optimize pricing, and enhance the guest experience.
